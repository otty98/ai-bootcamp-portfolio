About Me

I'm a full-stack software developer specializing in enterprise web applications using modern technologies including Angular, React, Node.js (Express & NestJS), and .NET. Currently working at Tiye Technologies, I build scalable solutions with microservices architecture, working across the entire development lifecycle from design to deployment. My technical expertise spans both frontend and backend development, with proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, and Python. I have hands-on experience with MySQL and PostgreSQL databases, automated testing using Selenium and Gauge, and DevOps practices including Docker and GitHub Actions. I've contributed to production systems for notable clients including AudioMind, MN Risk Services, and the City of Johannesburg's Asset Management Application. As a WeThinkCode_ graduate and CAPACITI trainee, I completed an intensive AI bootcamp where I gained practical skills in AI foundations, generative AI, machine learning, and ethical AI development. This unique combination allows me to approach problems with both traditional software engineering rigor and an understanding of emerging AI capabilities.

My Projects

My journey into artificial intelligence began with an intensive AI Bootcamp that provided a robust foundation in both theoretical and practical applications of modern AI. The curriculum focused heavily on Generative AI and Machine Learning, where I gained hands-on experience with models for natural language processing, computer vision, and machine learning. A core component of this training was ethical AI development, which involved auditing models for bias and developing mitigation strategies. Through various projects, I learned to build and deploy real-world AI applications, including custom content generators, chatbots, and AI-powered data analytics tools.
